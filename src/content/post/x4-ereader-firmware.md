Been flashing alternative firmwares on the X4 Xteink e-ink reader — a $70 device that turns out to be a surprisingly capable computing surface once you get past the stock interface.

Four different firmwares, four completely different personalities. The home screens alone tell the story — nothing carries over between them. The standout is Biscuit, which runs a WiFi scanner on the e-paper display. Watching SSIDs populate on e-ink is striking in a way that's hard to describe. It makes people do a double-take.

Building a shot list for an article on the whole thing. The Game Boy shot — Pokémon running on a $70 e-reader — is the one that will do the most work on social. Complete story in a single image.
