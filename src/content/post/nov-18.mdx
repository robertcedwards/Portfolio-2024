# Weeknotes: November 19th

I publish weeknotes every Tuesday covering my current projects, interesting discoveries, and ongoing experiments in AI research and development.

## Longevity AI & Digital Preservation

Recent conversations about AI have sparked interesting thoughts about its potential role in preserving digital culture and personal memories. Rather than replacing human interaction, the focus is on:

- Preserving memories of loved ones
- Creating digital legacies while people are still alive
- Enabling meaningful interactions with historical figures
- Building personal digital archives for future conversations

### Project Implementation

Using ElevenLabs' new Conversational AI (Beta), I've created two interactive experiences:

1. **Major General Norman B. Edwards Biography**
   - Built using digitized memoir audio cassettes
   - Custom voice cloning from historical recordings
   - Interactive bio page hosted on Hugging Face Spaces
   Try it here: [Norman Edwards Interactive Bio](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hashbrown/norman-b-edwards)

2. **Dr. James Hollis Dialogue Experience**
   - Based on his published works and lectures
   - Explores Jungian psychology and personal development
   - Custom voice implementation
   Experience it here: [James Hollis Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hashbrown/Dr-James-Hollis-Demo)
